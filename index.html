Signal

A synthesizer to learn by patching, and a master class in making music from nothing. Cables, knobs, your voice through pedals and a looper, and your computer’s keyboard as the piano. Everything stays in this browser.

The Ghost Producer

It asks questions about your track and leaves notes; it never plays or places a note. It uses your own key, kept in this browser and sent only to Anthropic. When you ask, your project's notes and numbers go there too.
